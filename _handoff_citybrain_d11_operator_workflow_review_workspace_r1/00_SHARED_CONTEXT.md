No dispatch, routing/control, enforcement, alerting-as-command, official ticket/case creation, legal/certified finding, public API, production readiness, identity/biometric inference, or model-generated factual claims without retained source evidence. `execution_state` remains not executed.

D10 is the intelligence-depth sprint: deterministic city-data search, expanded WATCH queries, selected-item content depth, field-computed RECALL match reasons, DIFF cadence start, Kit runtime probe, and standing D9 capability regression.
