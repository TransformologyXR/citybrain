# Known Limitations — R17

- BMD-45 is an offline CCTV-like frame dataset, not a live camera feed.
- Dataset annotations are reference labels, not official city records.
- DeepStream detections are sensor-inferred candidate observations only.
- Static-frame replay may require a generated MP4/image sequence workaround.
- IoU comparison is review evidence, not detector certification.
- No production monitoring, enforcement, ticketing, dispatch, legal finding, or identity inference is claimed.
