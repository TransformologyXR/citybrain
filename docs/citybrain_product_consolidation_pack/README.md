## Clean product truth

CityBrain is a governed review-only city intelligence cockpit. It helps humans understand, validate, brief, review and eventually approve city workflows under explicit authority. It is not yet a production live city operating system, autonomous dispatch platform, legal finding engine or official ticketing system.
