# Boundary Policy

This package audits cross-city domain fuel. It must not make any source into official Dubai truth.

Allowed:

- Read existing CityBrain outputs and manifests.
- Count and classify source material by city and domain.
- Create Seed R3 preflight plan.
- Identify donor/context distributions for synthetic Dubai-style scenarios.

Not allowed:

- Mutating prior seed/product/runtime outputs.
- Writing raw bulky source payloads into package.
- Writing secrets.
- Including human/person-level records.
- Promoting donor city data to Dubai fact.
- Claiming live monitoring, public API, production frontend, dispatch, control, enforcement, legal finding, or certified fact.
