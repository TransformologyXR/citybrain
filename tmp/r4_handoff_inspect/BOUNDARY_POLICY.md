# BOUNDARY POLICY — R4

## Product boundary

This is a bounded local/dev media-evidence review package.

It is not:

- production monitoring
- live CCTV operation
- legal violation detection
- official case management
- dispatch/routing/control/enforcement
- biometric/identity recognition
- automated action

## Source-class boundary

```text
DeepStream / Metropolis structured metadata = sensor_inferred
VSS narration = model_generated_narrative
Official records = not created in this lane
```

VSS may narrate R2 candidate evidence, but it may not become evidence truth.

## Required visible label

```text
Candidate observation
VSS narration only
Human review required
Not a finding
No action taken
```

## Human review boundary

Every packet must remain review-only. Human review is required before any interpretation outside the candidate-observation lane.
